Learning that lasts

A short reading guide · 阅读与记忆

Understanding a new idea is only the beginning. To remember it, we need opportunities to retrieve it. When you close a book and explain a concept in your own words, you are practising retrieval.

01 · Make the first encounter meaningful

New vocabulary is easier to understand when it belongs to a sentence. The word resilient, for example, describes something that can recover after difficulty. A resilient learner returns to a challenging problem and tries a different approach.

把词放回原句中理解，通常比孤立地看中文释义更有帮助。在 VLMReader 中，用鼠标选中一个词或一句话，右侧会显示释义，同时将所选内容和原句保存到生词本。

02 · Recall before you reveal

Looking at an answer can feel familiar, but familiarity is not the same as recall. Pause before revealing a translation. Try to remember the meaning, say it aloud, or write it down. Then check the answer and correct your understanding.

复习时先尝试回忆，再看答案。根据真实的回忆难度选择「重学、困难、记得、轻松」，系统据此安排下一次复习。

03 · Spread practice over time

Distributed practice gives memory several chances to develop. Short sessions on different days are often more useful for long-term retention than repeating the same material many times in one sitting.

无需一次背完所有词。先复习已经到期的条目，再学少量新词；保留原句，帮助你记住这个词在真实阅读中怎样使用。

04 · Read at your own pace

Choose a comfortable theme, adjust the text size, and listen when it helps. Reading is a conversation with an idea. You can move slowly through a difficult paragraph and quickly through one you already understand.

默认的离线语音支持中英文。播放速度可以连续调整。PDF 的文字层支持划词；纯图片扫描 PDF 需要先进行 OCR，才可以选中文字。

Notes for your next session

What was the main idea? Which sentence was difficult? What can you explain without looking? A few honest questions can turn reading into learning.
